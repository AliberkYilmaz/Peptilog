# Privacy Policy

**Peptilog** ("we", "us", "our")
Last updated: 2026-04-24

---

## 1. Introduction

Peptilog is a personal health tracking application for iOS and Android ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we store and use it, and the rights you have under applicable law including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK).

---

## 2. Data We Collect

When you use Peptilog we may process the following categories of personal data:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Account data | Email address, hashed password |
| Injection logs | Peptide compound, dose (mcg/mg), injection site, date and time |
| Weight logs | Body weight measurements with timestamps |
| Sleep logs | Sleep duration, sleep quality notes |
| Blood pressure logs | Systolic, diastolic, heart rate readings |
| Peptide library | Peptide names, vial sizes, concentration values |
| Reminders | Scheduled injection reminder times and associated peptides |

We do **not** collect advertising identifiers, device contacts, location data, or any data unrelated to health tracking.

---

## 3. How We Store Your Data

### 3.1 Local storage
All data is first written to an encrypted on-device database (Isar) stored in your device's private app storage. This data never leaves your device unless you opt in to cloud sync.

### 3.2 Cloud sync
If you create an account and sign in, your data is optionally synchronised to **Supabase** hosted in the **EU (Frankfurt, eu-central-1)** region. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and encrypted at rest using AES-256.

### 3.3 No third-party advertising
We do not share, sell, or license your data to advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for commercial purposes.

---

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:

- **Contractual necessity** — providing the features you requested (account, sync).
- **Consent** — you explicitly consent to data processing on first launch.
- **Legitimate interests** — security, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

---

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR / KVKK)

You have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate data.
- **Erasure ("right to be forgotten")** — delete your account and all associated data from our servers (available in-app via **Profile → Delete Account**).
- **Data portability** — export all your data as CSV (available in-app via **Profile → Export Data**).
- **Restriction** — request that we restrict processing of your data.
- **Withdraw consent** — at any time without affecting prior processing.

To exercise any right, contact us at **support@peptilog.com**.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- **Active accounts:** Data is retained while your account is active.
- **Deleted accounts:** All cloud data is permanently deleted within 30 days of an account deletion request. Local on-device data is removed when you delete the app.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Peptilog is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

We will notify users of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where required, by in-app notification.
